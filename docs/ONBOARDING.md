# Resident & Faculty Onboarding: QI Chief Tracker

Welcome to the **QI Chief Tracker**! This tool is designed to help you manage, track, and visualize your Quality Improvement (QI) projects with ease.

## Getting Started

### 1. View Your Projects

- Navigate to the **Projects** tab to see the Masterlist.
- Use the **Search** and **Filter** bars to find specific projects by title, status, or category (Inpatient vs. Outpatient).

### 2. Creating a New Project

- Click the **+ New Project** button on the Dashboard.
- Fill in the essential details: Title, Category, Faculty Mentor, Lead(s), and Proponents.
- **Note**: Ensure you follow the Zero-PHI policy—never enter patient names or MRNs.

### 3. Updating Progress

- Open a project detail page to see its **PDSA Cycle** and history.
- Click **Edit Project** to update the status (Idea → Pre-Intervention → Ongoing → Sustained).
- Add **Updates and Barriers** to keep the Chief and Mentors informed.

### 4. Tracking Metrics

- In the project view, you can enter monthly data points to generate charts.
- These charts help visualize your "before" and "after" intervention results.

## Best Practices

- **Updates**: Aim to update your project status at least once a month.
- **Leads**: Always specify at least one Lead Proponent for accountability.
- **Outcome**: Clearly define your "Primary Outcome" so stakeholders know what you're measuring.

## Need Help?

Contact your QI Chief or Faculty Mentor if you have questions about project methodology or database access.
